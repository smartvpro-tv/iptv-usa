# How to choose an IPTV service in the USA: a practical 2026 checklist

*Written by the team behind [SmartVPro](https://smartvpro.com/). We sell a subscription, so we are not neutral. This is the checklist we would give a friend in the US who asked which IPTV service to pick, and it works with any seller, including us.*

Last updated: November 2026

Download this checklist as a PDF: [iptv-usa-checklist-2026.pdf](https://smartvpro1.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/iptv-usa-checklist-2026.pdf)

## The short version

| Check | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Internet | About 10 Mbps per HD stream, about 25 Mbps per 4K stream |
| Device and app | Your device is supported, and you know which app you will use |
| Payment | A method you can dispute, such as PayPal |
| Trial | 24 hours on your own device and your own connection |
| Peak time | Stable during a big game, not only on a weekday morning |
| Commitment | One month first, longer only once you trust it |
| Refund | A written refund policy you have read |
| Rules | You know how to tell a legitimate service from the rest |

## 1. Check your internet first

As a rule of thumb, plan for about 10 Mbps per HD stream and about 25 Mbps per 4K stream. A wired connection or 5 GHz Wi-Fi fixes more buffering than changing provider does.

More detail: [internet speed you need](README.md#internet-speed-you-need) and our [buffering fixes, in the right order](https://smartvpro.com/iptv-buffering-fix/).

## 2. Check your device and app

Most services run on Fire TV Stick, Android TV, Samsung and LG smart TVs, Apple TV, PC and Mac, through apps such as IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate. Ask which apps are supported before you pay.

Device by device: [devices Americans use for IPTV](README.md#devices-americans-use-for-iptv).

## 3. Check how you pay

Pay with a method you can dispute if something goes wrong. If a provider only takes crypto, ask why. We take Venmo, PayPal, Amazon Pay and Google Pay.

## 4. Check what the trial really tests

A sales page tells you very little. A short trial on your own device and your own connection tells you most of what you need. We do not offer a free trial; ours is $2 for 24 hours on one device: [IPTV trial](https://smartvpro.com/iptv-trial/).

## 5. Check it at peak time

Run your trial during a big game, not on a weekday morning. Demand spikes on the server side on Sundays and during playoffs, and that is when weak services fail.

## 6. Check the lineup and the commitment

Ask for the full channel list, including your local affiliates and regional sports networks, before you pay. Then start with one month and go longer once you trust the service.

For reference, ours: 42,000+ live channels and 125,000+ VOD titles. Plans run 1, 3, 6 or 12 months for 1 or 2 connections, from $14.99 for one month on one connection to $64.99 for 12 months.

## 7. Check the refund policy

Read it before you pay, not after. Ours covers eligible purchases for 3 days: [refund policy](https://smartvpro.com/refund-policy-for-smartvpro/).

## 8. Check that the provider is legitimate

Read up on the rules before you buy. We wrote a plain guide: [how to verify a provider is legit](https://smartvpro.com/how-to-tell-if-ilegal-iptv-service/).
